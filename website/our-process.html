Our process

Four Steps to Your Maximum Financial Potential

We help clients create more wealth with less risk, at no additional out-of-pocket cost — so you reach a greater Maximum Financial Potential and can spend and enjoy more in retirement, with peace of mind that you won't run out of money.

01
Start here

Get on the Gameboard

Most people arrive with a financial junk drawer — products bought from different people, at different times, for different reasons, all sitting in silos instead of working as one coordinated strategy. We start by putting everything on the table and filling out your LEAP Model: every account, policy, and dollar of cash flow, in one place.

You'll walk away with your Maximum Financial Potential (MFP) Report, including:

  • →A plain-language narrative of your present position — where you stand today
  • →Your current Maximum Financial Potential and projected retirement income, based on what you're doing right now
  • →A Problem ID worksheet and a resource identification worksheet
  • →Your potential MFP and retirement-income increase if you work with us
02

Optimize Your Cashflow

Once we can see the board, we optimize it. Using the Currence system, we find the cash flow that's currently leaking or sitting idle, and redirect it toward your goals — without asking you to cut your lifestyle or take on more risk.

03

Build Your TurboCharger

Next we build your TurboCharger: a life-insurance-based volatility buffer that protects you against market downturns and increases your income in retirement. Think of it as a covered-asset strategy — a way to keep more of what the market gives you, and lose less of what it takes away.

04

Lock in Your Legacy

Finally, we lock in your legacy. Working with our estate planning partners and MyLegacyLock, we make sure what you've built is protected and passes down the way you intend — wisdom and structure, not just assets.

The problems

Three Problems Most Americans Face

Financial Junk Drawer

We bought all these things from different people at different times under different circumstances for different reasons. Now we have siloed information like a 401k from two jobs ago, a policy somebody sold us, a brokerage account we don't even check half the time. They're not working together.

Financial Treadmill

Keeps people in a space where they live in unconscious consumption while trying to manually save, which creates lifestyle creep in somebody's financial life. This is why most Americans are saving 4% of their income and not being prepared for retirement.

Financial Information Overload

We have so much financial information bombarding us throughout our life. It's hard to understand what's right for us and what's not, and what's a full strategy vs. just a snippet online.

How the System Gets Built

Four steps that make up your Cash Flow Operating System. No products until the structure is on paper.

01
Organize It

Your income is separated, your accounts are in one clear structure, and smart automations are in place so every dollar has a job.

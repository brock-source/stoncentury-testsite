About

Built by Someone Who's Lived Both Sides of Wealth

I started StoneCentury because I saw too many high earners struggling with no way to test or verify their financial decisions. My father trained advisors in the LEAP method — I grew up around the idea that structure can be proven on paper, not just promised.

I've been broke, and I've built businesses. Nine years in, I've helped hundreds of high earners turn income into peace of mind, systems, and legacy. Most people don't need more income. They need a better system — and I'd rather you understand yours than delegate it to me.

Freedom without safeguards is only temporary.

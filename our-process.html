Our process

Four Steps to Your Maximum Financial Potential

We help clients create more wealth with less risk, at no additional out-of-pocket cost — so you reach a greater Maximum Financial Potential and can spend and enjoy more in retirement, with peace of mind that you won't run out of money.

Together, these four steps make up your Cash Flow Operating System — and you'll follow every one of them live in your client portal.

01
Start here

Organize It

We start by getting everything in one place. Your income is separated, your accounts are pulled into one clear structure, and smart automations are in place so every dollar has a job.

You'll walk away with your Maximum Financial Potential (MFP) Report, including:

  • →A plain-language narrative of your present position — where you stand today
  • →Your current Maximum Financial Potential and projected retirement income, based on what you're doing right now
  • →A Problem ID worksheet and a resource identification worksheet
  • →Your potential MFP and retirement-income increase if you work with us
02

Track It

Your Cash Flow Operating System gives you a real-time feedback loop, so you can see where money is leaking and know when to invest and what to invest. It's a whole lot easier to fill a bucket with holes when you plug the leaks rather than add more water or speed up the water.

03

Protect It

Protect what you've built against the three catastrophic risks of life—viability, disability, and premature death—so a single event can't undo years of progress.

04

Build It

Reach your maximum financial potential so you can spend and enjoy the maximum amount without fear of running out of money where all dollars are doing more than one job.
